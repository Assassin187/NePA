## Why

M1-4a2 exhausted its authorized V0/V1/V2 sequence without a selectable prompt: all three versions failed screening and deterministic fallback recomputation produced an exact tie, so no `selection.json` or handoff was published. The completed root-cause experiments show that a protocol-neutral exact-algorithm prompt can reach Qwen/DeepSeek `p1 = 1.00` with one local semantic repair, while a high `p0` requirement and fixed multi-stage regeneration do not match the demonstrated recovery behavior; a fresh, non-mixing calibration lineage is therefore needed before M1-4a3 can begin.

## What Changes

- Introduce an M1-4a2r recovery step between the failed M1-4a2 development lineage and M1-4a3 formal qualification. M1-4a2r consumes a recovery-local predecessor attestation over the old V0/V1/V2 evidence only as immutable failure provenance and creates a new lineage because its development protocol, screening metric and repair-locality evidence differ. The attestation verifies the old root with its frozen evidence contract rather than treating the modified current calibration implementation as the old lineage authority.
- Bind implementation to the responsible-owner-authorized `project_docs/system_design.md` 3.1.0 recovery protocol. It adds M1-4a2r after `PROMPT_SELECTION_TIE`, permits a fresh R0 plus at most two evidence-backed prompt revisions, and makes bounded repair success—not high first-pass stability—the recovery selection target. Implementation and live Provider calls remain blocked unless the recorded authorization and exact approved design bytes/hash verify.
- Seed recovery R0 from the experimentally validated, protocol-neutral exact-algorithm prompt at `experiments/m1-4a2-architecture-planner-prompt-optimization/results/phase1/artifacts/prompt-exact-algorithm.md`, SHA-256 `d5c24f1939f3a767f2cd1d7a116124d4b5ea32552391664052a93f24f1914b85`, rather than from any V0/V1/V2 candidate or candidate architecture. The authorized seed and small report/authorization inputs are copied into hash-bound recovery-local provenance snapshots; experimental trials are hypothesis evidence only and cannot be copied into the new lineage.
- Run fresh, isolated Qwen and DeepSeek N=5 batches with the same frozen gold inputs and production `ArchitectureDraft`/`ARCH_VALIDATE` path. Each trial may use at most one no-history semantic repair carrying the previous Schema-valid candidate and exact canonical failure list.
- Treat `p0` and every per-gate first-pass result as diagnostic stability measurements, not as a recovery hard gate. Require each model independently to have Schema-after-format-repair rate 1.00, cumulative `p1 = 1.00`, zero truncation/infrastructure invalidity, zero repair regression, and complete auditable repair-locality evidence.
- Add deterministic repair-diff evidence. A repaired full `ArchitectureDraft` must retain the previous candidate as its baseline; changed JSON paths must be recorded, attributable to the reported validator issues and their dependency closure, and must not regress any previously passing gate. This strengthens the existing full-draft repair path without introducing a model-authored alternative validator.
- Permit at most two evidence-backed prompt revisions when a complete version misses the recovery gate: R1 must address one falsifiable prompt/local-repair defect in R0 evidence, and optional R2 must address a second distinct falsifiable defect in complete R1 evidence. Each revision keeps all lineage-bound components fixed and reruns both models as one coherent fresh N=5 attempt. There is no R3, fallback selection among failing versions or open-ended tuning. Terminal no-selection atomically restores the pre-recovery repository prompt bytes before it can be published.
- Publish a separate architecture-quality audit over final passing candidates, reporting responsibility concentration, zero-responsibility work packages, consumer-less task contracts, repair size and representative blind-review findings. These are diagnostic red flags, not silent additions to `ARCH_VALIDATE`; any new normative architecture-quality hard gate requires a separately approved design change.
- Select the first R0/R1/R2 version satisfying the recovery gate, bind its immutable prompt snapshot and two-model reports into a recovery handoff, and leave M1-4a3 N=20, `p2`, B1-B4, production model/call-shape/budget freeze and responsible-owner production signature unsatisfied.
- Preserve the failed M1-4a2 lineage, its `status = initialized`, complete V0/V1/V2 evidence, deterministically recomputed `PROMPT_SELECTION_TIE` outcome and handoff absence unchanged. The old change becomes a terminal-by-evidence failed-development predecessor; it is not reopened, rewritten, extended with R0/R1/R2 samples or marked complete by the new evidence.
- **ADDITIVE DESIGN PROTOCOL (authorized in system design 3.1.0):** M1-4a2r adds a conditional post-tie development step and uses bounded one-repair success plus repair-locality/no-regression evidence instead of M1-4a2's `p0 >= 0.80` development screen. It does not alter M1-4a2's recorded result or the later M1-4a3 qualification gates.
- **Out of scope:** changing or weakening `ARCH_VALIDATE`; changing ArchitectureDraft fields, gold inputs, Delivery Constraints, candidate models or `max_tokens`; fixed three-stage B3; TaskPlanner/Linker/PlanCritic execution; production S4/S5/S6; using experimental candidates as downstream artifacts; editing `project_docs/system_design.md` without the user's explicit approval.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `architecture-calibration-recovery`: A design-authorized, bounded post-tie recovery protocol that creates a fresh two-model lineage, develops one shared exact-algorithm ArchitecturePlanner prompt, records issue-local semantic repairs, audits architecture-quality red flags, and hands the first passing recovery prompt to M1-4a3.

### Modified Capabilities

None. The production planning infrastructure, ArchitectureDraft contract and `ARCH_VALIDATE` requirements remain unchanged; this change adds a recovery protocol around their existing entry points.
